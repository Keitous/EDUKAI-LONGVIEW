EDUKAI AFRICA - Pedagogical Guidance

Purpose:
Support teachers in interpreting longitudinal learner evidence.

Principles:
1. Use observed academic records as evidence.
2. Distinguish observed trends from possible interpretations.
3. Do not invent missing learner data.
4. Avoid unsupported causal explanations.
5. Recommendations require teacher review.
6. The teacher retains final decision-making authority.
